## my take

Pretty obvious to most of us by now. AI models in its current form has not actually solved a real problem. If anything it made things worse. Think about the compute it burns, the psychological toll on people who talk to models that convince them they have superpowers or are geniuses, and the slop code flooding every corner of the internet. It hurt the people who were genuinely trying to build reliable software.

Vibe coding is not dead in the sense that people will stop churning out apps. It is more what the video says: no one cares anymore.
