## my take

It's sad seeing hardworking students studying for 4-5 years, working and doing internships, then getting rejected because of capitalism and government greed. I see many of my friends in this video. Many of them ask me to help find a summer job or full-time position, not because they aren't trying, but because they've started losing hope in a world that only cares about power and money.

> "Capitalism is working to produce more money but does not see the people. This problem is getting worse across the world.", Lech Walesa

I don't fully agree that coding will fully disappear. AI companies will make it sound that way. But coding is really an ART, and only people who love it and have taste will understand that. Only those people will keep coding no matter what.
